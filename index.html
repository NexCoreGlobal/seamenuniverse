The World

Technology Failed.
Leadership Must Prevail.

2047. The Abyss — an alien civilization dormant for millennia in the Mariana Trench — awakens and disables every satellite, drone, and AI system on Earth in 72 hours. Humanity's most advanced naval forces collapse. There are no commanders left who can lead a full-scale naval engagement by instinct alone.

🌊

The Last Frontier

Humanity mastered land and sky through AI-driven warfare. The ocean — ancient, unpredictable — was the one frontier that never yielded to technology.

⚡

System Collapse

Abyss Bio-Frequency disables all satellites, drones, and AI simultaneously. Every modern weapon is blind. The battlefield returns to instinct, tide, and wind.

🧬

Project Legend

Humanity's last resort: DNA resurrection and memory reconstruction of history's two greatest naval strategists. East meets West on the final battlefield.

The Enemy

Not a Conqueror.
A Refugee Civilization.

The Abyss are not villains — they are survivors. Defeated in an interplanetary war, they fled across the galaxy and chose Earth's ocean as their final sanctuary. For thousands of years they lay dormant, evolving, watching humanity grow. In 2047, they are ready.

  • 01Defeated in their home planet's war — the last survivors of a dying civilization
  • 02Chose Earth's deep ocean as refuge, not conquest — "a darkness to flee to"
  • 03Fused with bedrock at 11,000m in the Mariana Trench for millennia
  • 04Bio-Frequency disables all satellites, drones & AI simultaneously
  • 05Non-linear, organic battle patterns — unpredictable by any algorithm
The Golden Turtle Ship

武器가 아닌, 象徵
Not a Weapon. A Symbol.

The Bukhyeonmu (北玄武) — the Black Tortoise of the North, one of the Four Guardians of East Asian mythology — manifests as the Golden Turtle Ship when Yi Sun-sin makes the ultimate sacrifice. It cannot be awakened by technology. Only by human will.
